The three financial statements

Every company tells its story in three documents. The income statement shows whether it made money. The balance sheet shows what it owns and owes on one day. The cash flow statement shows where the cash actually went.

THE ONE RULE
Assets = Liabilities + Equity
A sari-sari store with ₱80,000 of stock and a ₱30,000 loan has ₱50,000 of owner's equity.

The three connect. Profit from the income statement flows into equity on the balance sheet, and the cash flow statement explains why cash changed even when profit did not.
