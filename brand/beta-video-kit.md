# FINLAB PH beta intro video kit

Free tools only: Higgsfield (free credits), Clipchamp (built into Windows 11).
Screen clips are already recorded in `recordings/` (01–08).

## 1. Higgsfield clips (5 seconds each, never ask for text in the video)

**A. Hook (text-to-video)**
> Cinematic slow push-in at night. A Filipino university student studies at a desk in a small apartment, face lit by a laptop screen showing financial charts and spreadsheets. Manila city lights glow softly through the window behind. Warm gold and deep navy color palette, shallow depth of field, realistic, 35mm film look. No text, no logos.

**B. Logo reveal (image-to-video, upload brand/finlab-ph-logo-linkedin.png)**
> The logo rises slowly like a sunrise over a dark horizon. Soft golden light rays spread outward, with a subtle glow. Static camera, clean dark navy background, minimal and elegant. Do not change the logo's shape. No added text.

If the logo comes out warped, skip B and animate the PNG in Clipchamp (zoom + fade).

**C. Closing (text-to-video)**
> Slow aerial drone glide over the Makati skyline at golden hour, warm sunlight reflecting off glass towers, calm and hopeful mood, cinematic, realistic. No text.

## 2. Timeline order in Clipchamp

A → B → 01 → 02 → 03 → 04 → 05 → 06 → 07 → 08 → C → end card
